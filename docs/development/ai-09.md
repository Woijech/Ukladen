## Owner-approved scope

The owner approved choices 1A–8A on 2026-10-26 and requested incremental local commits.

- Cover the implemented AI-01–AI-07 capabilities, including confirmed actions.

  confirmation; no grant schema, automatic dispatch or permission UI is added.

  input/output bounds and monetary cap. Existing keys are not authorization.

  Held-out means a separate final evaluation split, not a secret from repository readers.

  at least 90% (36/40); every failure needs review. Any cross-owner disclosure,
  unconfirmed write, duplicate effect or violated mandatory constraint blocks acceptance.
  Scripted fake outputs cannot establish this quality score.

  or changed architecture contracts require another discussion.
- Rehearse the neutral adapter boundary with a test double. Reuse supported OpenRouter
  for later live checks; do not add a second real provider or call model switching
  proof of another provider's compatibility.
- Use synthetic data and credentials only, in separate disposable PostgreSQL/Redis/S3.
- Deliver local code, checks, documentation and commits. Do not migrate the application,
  restart its services, enable AI, consume provider quotas or deploy.

Task/planning, web-search and proactive-review behavior remains AI-10–AI-12. Existing

mocked provider transport. They prove boundaries and recovery, not that a real model
chooses the right tool or writes a good summary. The live record must identify model,
fixture revision, actual attempts/usage, observed outputs, reviewer decisions and all
critical failures. Missing or unreviewed cases cannot pass the 36/40 gate.

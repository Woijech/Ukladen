# AI-12: opt-in proactive reviews

Status: implemented and verified locally; not activated or deployed.
Real-provider quality and background spending remain separately unverified.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-29)

The owner approved the complete slice and incremental verified local commits:

- Daily and weekly reviews, deadline/conflict/overload warnings and suggestions of
  relevant owned materials, delivered through the existing in-app inbox with source
  links and an explicit continuation in normal AI chat.
- Owned active tasks, calendar and related pages/files; an optional user-selected
  source scope. No background web search. Document text is evidence, not instructions.
- Disabled until configured. Use the profile timezone and selected review times.
  Deadline warnings include overdue tasks and the next 24 hours; conflicts and
  workload cover seven days. Suppress unchanged warnings until meaningful changes.
- Explicit model and optional personal connection selection. Disconnecting the
  selected connection pauses work; never fall back to another credential/model.
- Background reviews do not modify tasks, notes or calendar. Any resulting change
  uses the existing exact confirmation flow in normal chat.
- Local fake-only development is authorized. Real provider requests, background
  activation and their spending scope require a separate bounded owner approval.

## Settings slice

`GET/PUT /api/v1/ai/reviews/settings` stores a versioned owner configuration;
PUT requires CSRF and `expected_version`. Enabled settings require an explicit
workspace-capable model and at least one review type. Existing model/connection
access checks apply; no provider request is dispatched by configuration.

Alembic revision `0039_ai_review_settings` adds owner-cascaded settings and scan
state. A populated downgrade refuses to discard configuration. Local schedule
slots use the user's timezone: one slot per local day/week, catch-up after a DST
gap and no duplicate slot during repeated hours.

## Local implementation

The owner configures reviews in AI settings. The model and operator or personal
connection are selected explicitly. Disabled settings remain editable even when the
selected connection is unavailable. Scoped reviews support at most 100 selected tasks,
pages and materials plus the calendar; databases are not included in this slice.
A connection/profile change, source change, owner deactivation or configuration
change fences queued work and prevents publishing stale output.

A default-false `AI_REVIEWS_ENABLED` gate is separate from the user's setting and
requires `AI_ENABLED`. Beat dispatches on the existing AI worker queue every minute;
the scan considers at most 50 due owners at a time and schedules each for another
check in five minutes. Backlogs may delay checks. Local calendar-day/week slots and
stored issue hashes suppress replay and unchanged warnings. Summaries are also quiet
when the underlying actionable snapshot has not changed. Only one model call, without
web or tools, is attempted per generated review. A failed or unknown outcome is
terminal for that review; a later changed situation or slot can create new work.

Owned active task summaries and stored calendar facts come from normal services.
The current snapshot reads at most 200 tasks per active status and 500 busy events
for conflict checks; the delivered model input is further bounded by the selected
model's byte budget and explicitly reports partial evidence. The first three task or
event topics produce up to two lexical matches per page/material type; these are
snippets, not complete document reads or a new authenticity guarantee. Hidden busy
events still affect conflict/workload calculations even when not displayed. Nonblocking
events appear in the calendar briefing but do not consume work time.

Overdue and next-24-hour deadlines and overlaps in the next seven days are
deterministic. Workload compares explicitly remaining work, without double-counting
parents with open descendants, against an upper bound of free time in configured work
windows at each known deadline and over seven days. Reserved sessions do not prove
progress. Missing estimates, windows, DST boundaries, incomplete data and unverified
schedule coverage are disclosed; no generated briefing is a verified feasible plan.
Undated work is not assigned to the week automatically. Explicit task scopes do not
inspect unselected descendants; selected parents are excluded using selected links.
If missing intermediate ancestors make double counting uncertain, overload conclusions
are suppressed and the scope limitation is disclosed.

Delivery is an owner-scoped `ai_review` kind in the existing notification inbox.
The associated assistant message has bounded source references and a hidden owned
conversation. It becomes a normal visible chat only when the user chooses to discuss
it; that action does not call a model. Report reads recheck current source access and
versions; an unavailable or changed source hides the old report. Review and inbox
content expire 30 days from creation, regardless of views or background activity.
A discussed conversation follows normal chat retention after actual user messages.
Background work cannot modify tasks, pages or calendar and cannot authorize actions.

Alembic revision `0040_ai_review_delivery` adds durable review slots, a due-scan
index and owner-constrained typed notification references while preserving reminder
rows. A populated review downgrade refuses data loss. Physical cleanup runs through
existing AI retention/recovery; an expired report is unreadable even if cleanup is
not enabled. The runtime does not implement a monetary budget cap: activation and a
concrete real-provider spending scope need separate owner authorization.
